# Screens spec (from the user's reference screenshots)
The reference game is a MECHANICS reference only. Same information and flow, but our own art, names, colours (dark wood, brass, parchment, teal, gold, indigo), Arabic RTL layout and our own arrangement where noted. Never reproduce its art, logo or exact layout.

## S1 Main menu
Full-screen dark sunset-orange background. Rough torn dark-red edge frame. Silhouettes of jinn/ghouls with glowing red eyes along the bottom. Hero art centre. Logo (app.title) top-centre, large. Three big wooden buttons at the bottom: left options, centre PLAY (biggest), right heroes. Version number tiny top-right. (Replaces "Other games" with heroes/chests/wheel shortcuts.)

## S2 Wave banner
At wave start (often at night): screen dims; a huge glowing-blue word `wave.banner` appears and below it the wave number in white; fades after 2 s.

## S3 Tutorial hints
A wide parchment scroll banner at the top-centre with 1-2 lines of text. Big bold red arrows at the left/right middle of the screen showing where to press; one arrow points at the relevant object (gate, button). Bottom-right arrow points at the ability button. Hints show on waves 1-2 only.

## S4 Battle HUD
Top-left: pause button (square, wood). Next to it: wave progress (skull-like jinn lamp icon + percentage bar). Top-centre: hero portrait with a sword-shaped health bar (green segments). Top-right: dirham count (silver icon) and dinar count (gold icon); under them the date (heal) button with a small red arrow indicating where enemies come from. Bottom-left: round haybah badge with the number inside, a long red segmented bar beside it (segments = power thresholds), and ally buttons with a small cost badge. Bottom-right: ability buttons (round, cooldown ring). In OUR version: hero HP top-left, wave bar top-centre, currencies + pause top-right, date button bottom-centre (see decisions.md).

## S5 Setup (3 pages)
Page title in a red/wood banner (Allies / Abilities / Charms). Left: big red parchment panel with a scroll frame; inside, a grid of unlocked items (icon + name). Under it: a row of round empty slots (allies 5, abilities 2, charms 1). Right: preview scene (night cave, enemies waiting, wave number on a wooden sign) with a speech-bubble description of the selected item. Between them a vertical strip with swirl ornaments. Bottom-left back arrow, bottom-right green check.

## S6 Victory
Large title "Wave N: Victory!" top-left. Rows: Victory award / Collected / Total, each with a coin icon and number. A horizontal row of reward cards: a coin card (amount) then charm cards with names (power, luck, friendship/brotherhood, wealth, haste, peace). Below: "You unlocked:" with the new ally/ability icon and its name. Top-right button for a free reward. Bottom-right green check.

## S7 Shop
Parchment background with red scroll banners top and bottom. Left vertical tabs (hero / allies / heroes / supplies), a yellow "!" on tabs with affordable items. Top-left: revive status ("no revive left"). Centre title SHOP. Top-right: dirham and dinar counts and a free-reward button. Grid 2 rows x 4. Each item: round icon with a brass ring, price (with currency icon) and name under it. Hero tab: free rewards, loot chest, level up hero / sword / bow / haybah (costs dinar) / gate / slash, then row of LOCKED abilities with a padlock and "unlocks at wave N". Allies tab: level up farmer, then locked allies with padlock, unlock wave and haybah-cost badge. Heroes tab: five silhouettes with question marks, "Find <name>!". Supplies tab: double dirhams forever, welcome/deal packs (we do NOT sell packs for real money), wheel spins, revive potion, date, date plate, coffee cup, coffee pot. Bottom: back arrow, wheel button, spin count, green check.

## S8 Wave results flow
After a wave: victory -> (shop) -> setup -> battle. Defeat: retry or menu.

## Unlock schedule (waves)
Allies: farmer 1, guard 4, archer 5, spearman 6, masked 14, sage 15, vizier 17, cavalry 23. Abilities: crescent slash start, arrow volley 2, hourglass 7, bell 11, seal 12, rage 13, tornado 20, sun burst 40.

## Excluded on purpose
Choose-your-side (zombie side) mode, real-money packs, VIP, ads, pachinko (replaced by the wheel of fortune).
